Headphones put music in your ears. We wanted it in the whole jacket.

Every Copper Frequency jacket is built on the same platform. A copper-plated conductive fabric is bonded under the shell and carries the circuit pattern you see on the outside. Flat tactile transducers sit in pockets against your chest, ribs and spine, and turn the low end of whatever you're playing into movement you can feel.

A small rechargeable pod drives the system and clips out when you wash the jacket. At the hem there's a frequency port. Plug in the meter and it shows the signal live, right on the jacket.

The shells are where each style gets its character: a white F1 racer, a black racer, a leather moto, an MA-1 bomber, an oversized punk jacket, a hooded technical shell and a tactical piece with a copper endoskeleton. Five of them come in a women's cut too.

      Risks and challenges

      Wearable electronics are harder to make than clothing or gadgets alone. Here's where things could slip, and what we're doing about it.

      Batteries and shipping

      Lithium cells need certified test reports before they can ship. We're only using cells and packs that already carry UN38.3 and IEC 62133 reports, and we're filing shipping paperwork during pre-production rather than after.

      Wash durability

      Copper traces have to survive washing and wear. The module clips out, every trace is sealed under film, and each production batch is wash-tested before it's allowed to ship.

      Twelve styles at once

      Running many styles can slow a first production run. All twelve share one electronics platform, and if timing gets tight, the racing block and bomber ship first while other styles follow, with updates to every backer.

      FAQ

      What does the jacket actually do?

      It's a wearable sound system for your body. Tactile transducers turn the bass in your music into vibration you feel through the jacket. Plug in your phone, a DJ mixer or an instrument and the jacket follows it.

      Is it a health or wellness device?

      No. Copper Frequency is clothing with built-in audio hardware. We don't make any medical or therapeutic claims about it.

      How do I wash it?

      Unclip the frequency module, close the pod pocket and hand-wash or use a cold delicate cycle, then hang dry. Leather and canvas styles get their own care instructions on the label.

      How long does the battery last?

      Our target is a full night out on one charge, about 8 hours at moderate levels. We'll publish measured battery life from pre-production samples during the campaign updates. It charges over USB-C.

      Can I choose my style and size later?

      Yes. After the campaign ends you'll get a survey to choose your style, line and size. We'll publish full size charts for each style before the survey goes out.

      Do you ship internationally?

      We plan to ship worldwide. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout on Kickstarter and any import duties are paid on delivery, depending on your country.

      What is the meter plug-in?

      A small light-bar meter that plugs into the frequency port at the hem. It reads the signal going to the transducers and lights up in time with it. It's removable, and every reward includes one.
