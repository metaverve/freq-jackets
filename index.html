    The collection

    Seven men's styles and five women's, each one built on the same copper platform. Five of them come as matched pairs.

    How it works

    Every style shares one platform, built in four layers from the outside in. Swap the shell and the wiring stays the same.

    1. Shell

      The outer jacket: racing twill, flight nylon, leather, canvas or a 3-layer technical shell. This is what changes from style to style.

    2. Copper weave

      A copper-plated conductive fabric bonded under the shell. It carries the circuit pattern you can see on the outside of each jacket.

    3. Transducers and harness

      Flat tactile transducers sit in pockets against the body, wired on a sealed low-voltage harness. They turn the low end of your music into movement.

    4. Frequency module and port

      A small rechargeable pod holds the amp and battery. It clips out so the jacket can be washed. The port at the hem takes a meter plug-in that shows the signal live.

    Made in pairs

    Five of the men's styles have a women's counterpart, cut for a different body with the same circuit. Back them together as a pair.
